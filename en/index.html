03About

Open standards for agentic AI, powered by local volunteers.

Foundation · AAIF

What is the Agentic AI Foundation?

The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) is a neutral, open foundation under the Linux Foundation hosting open protocols and open-source projects for agentic AI, along with working groups on reliability, security & privacy, observability, identity & trust, and workflows.

About AAIF (aaif.io) (opens in new tab) AAIF GitHub (opens in new tab)

Community · China

What does AAIF Chinese Community do?

We are an open, volunteer-run community across Chinese cities (not an official foundation website) committed to vendor-neutral technical exchange. We host in-person meetups in Beijing, Shenzhen, Shanghai, Hangzhou, and beyond to help builders move open agentic standards from demo to production.

Community GitHub (opens in new tab) Site repository (opens in new tab)

Key open-source projects hosted by AAIF

All official projects (opens in new tab)
  • MCP Model Context Protocol Open protocol for seamless integration between LLM applications, tools, and external data sources. (opens in new tab)
  • A2A Agent2Agent Open protocol enabling AI agents to communicate and collaborate securely across platforms and frameworks. (opens in new tab)
  • AGENTS.md Coding Agent format A simple, open format for giving coding agents project context, rules, and instructions. (opens in new tab)
  • Agent Skills Reusable expertise An open format for packaging and sharing reusable agent domain expertise and workflows. (opens in new tab)
  • Goose Extensible AI agent Open-source, extensible AI agent that installs, edits, executes, and tests with any LLM. (opens in new tab)
  • agentgateway Agentic AI gateway Secure, scalable connectivity between AI agents, models, tools, and APIs across ecosystems. (opens in new tab)

Additional AAIF-hosted projects include OpenSandbox, Open Responses, OpenEnv, and Agent Router.

Official title

Community Organizer

社区活动组织者

We are volunteers organizing local AAIF meetups across Chinese cities. Our official title is Community Organizer. This site lists publicly announced events, shares open collaboration guides, and connects Chinese builders with the global AAIF ecosystem.

04Join

Join AAIF Chinese Community,
connect and organize together.

  1. 01

    Attend meetups & follow city calendars

    Register for upcoming in-person events, or subscribe to your local chapter's Luma calendar to receive announcements first.

    Upcoming events City calendars

  2. 02

    Join local Chapter WeChat groups

    Beijing, Shenzhen, Shanghai, and Hangzhou each maintain a local community group. Please note "AAIF Chinese Community" when requesting to join. To prevent spam, group QR codes (7-day validity) are refreshed regularly and shared at local meetups or via chapter organizers.

  3. 03

    Apply to organize in your city

    If your city does not yet have an AAIF chapter, apply directly to the Agentic AI Foundation to become a local Community Organizer.
