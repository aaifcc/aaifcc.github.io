# AAIF Chinese Community

> Volunteer-run AAIF Chinese Community site organizing open meetups and technical events across Chinese cities around open agentic AI standards (MCP, AGENTS.md, Goose, Agent Skills, etc.).
>
> **Disclaimer**: This is a volunteer-maintained community site. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Agentic AI Foundation (a Linux Foundation project). The official foundation website is <https://aaif.io/>.
>
> **Official Title**: Community Organizer (`社区活动组织者`). Community volunteers do **not** use the title "Ambassador".

## Overview

- Community: AAIF Chinese Community (`AAIF 中文社区`)
- Official Foundation Website: <https://aaif.io/>
- Domain Sponsor: [vivo](https://github.com/vivo)

  - Chinese HTML: `/` · Chinese Markdown: `/index.md`
  - English HTML: `/en/` · English Markdown: `/en/index.md`
  - Agent Discovery File: `/llms.txt`
- Time Zone: All event times below are in Beijing Time (UTC+8).
